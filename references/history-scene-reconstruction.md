# Reconstructing documented scenes

Read [scripting DNA](scripting-dna.md), [evidence boundaries](historical-fiction.md) and the selected live channel. This reference supports documentary events and processes. The former baseline/intervention/aftermath specialist is [archived](../config/archive/dna-leak-recheck-2026-10-16/references/history-scene-reconstruction.md).

## Scene ledger

For each meaningful passage record time/place, starting state, supported action or process, changed state, what participants could know, source locator and uncertainty. An event can change position, visibility, water level, access, available choices or consequences. A scene does not require an invented decision or person.

Orient the viewer before an action depends on geography or a technical condition. Explain a mechanism where it changes what happens. Track the same vehicle, water route, place or service comparison through the change. Source outcomes and limits; do not invent private thoughts, dialogue, testimony or an intervention to connect missing evidence.

For war, orient parties, approach, contact, response, communications and actual outcome. Maps explain the geometry; supported crew/combat actions show its consequences. Keep harm non-graphic and humane. A local incident does not establish why a whole war was won.

## Narration and visual handoff

Write the continuous narration before shot directions. Keep image-production notes and source administration outside speech. A diagram may explain an otherwise invisible relationship; it does not need a fictional operator. Do not extend a passage by repeating its mechanism or appending a generic lesson.

After narration approval, follow [visual DNA](visual-dna.md), [beat layer](beat-layer.md), [animation direction](animation-direction.md) and [image reuse](image-reuse.md). Describe the supported action/state, exact useful in-scene labels, scale and continuity. Freeze essential action in `animation.keyframe`; the still renderer adds camera movement and transitions, not physical events. Prompt export alone is not visual review.

## Specifically selected scenarios

Only when the episode explicitly selects a counterfactual, follow [documentary and fiction boundaries](historical-fiction.md). Record baseline, divergence and consequential assumptions separately. Use `scenario_context` for the authored branch where needed. A documentary does not acquire an intervention or alternate aftermath because it uses reconstruction.
