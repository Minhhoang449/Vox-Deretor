# Retention audit for channel documentaries

Run after story edit on the actual version. Record question, channel grammar, opening promise and final payoff. A text audit is not retention analytics.

Judge progress in the viewer's understanding, whether the selected shape is a focused task or connected routines. A day is not a fixed checklist. Do not require a crisis or difficulty in the opening. Merge repeated qualifications only when they add no new scope or inference; keep material uncertainty naturally at its claim. Topic-market readiness belongs in topic-selection.md, not a retention score inferred from public views.

Use [viewer-guidance.md](viewer-guidance.md) as the cognitive-psychology reference. Begin with the title/thumbnail expectation and check whether the opening image and first spoken line confirm it. Mark the viewer's first useful progress by roughly 0:30, the point YouTube uses for its intro metric. Treat this as an editorial checkpoint, not a fixed hook formula.

Map natural passages by viewer question, useful answer/change, evidence and next dependency. Find the first useful answer, the strongest mechanism reveal and any stretch where understanding stalls. Check geography/time/scale transitions. Do not add fixed tease, joke or chapter intervals.

For each major passage, write: active question → evidence/state change → updated understanding → next question or payoff. Partial answers should arrive when they are supported; keep the central question active by advancing its evidence, not by withholding an answer or adding a false cliffhanger. Check whether terms, numbers and images help a new viewer build the current process model. In an image-led sequence, a still change should mark a new fact, state, scale or task; motion alone is not progress.

State what the viewer can now explain, distinguish or predict after each meaningful passage. Flag adjacent passages with the same result even if their vocabulary, study or diagram changes. Check whether moving from one source summary to another actually changes the episode's explanation. Preserve necessary scope and orientation; no fixed rate of novelty is required. If a comparison was promised, locate its actual bounded outcome instead of accepting a general mechanism as its payoff. Shorter narration and a higher self-score do not establish attention or retention.

Score 0–2 separately:
1. Concrete opening earns the correct channel-specific curiosity.
2. Understanding progresses through events, flows, comparisons or action without repetition.
3. Natural sections pay their promises and connect causally.
4. Mechanisms, important numbers and uncertainty are visually explainable.
5. Ending answers the central question and delivers the relevant human/system consequence or limit.

Revise every zero and the highest-risk quoted passages. Report /10 separately from story edit /16, actual revisions and limits. No compulsory mission announcement, traveler, alternate result or fictional future. After publication inspect comparable videos' actual intro retention, dips, spikes, traffic-source CTR and watch time. Compare similar lengths and traffic sources where possible. A spike may show interest, sharing or confusion; inspect the exact segment before inferring a cause. Record observed analytics separately from editorial hypotheses. If analytics are unavailable, say so; do not estimate retention from scores, public views or runtime.

## Execution check — 17/10/2026

Apply the Story execution section in scripting-dna.md. Record the actual speech source and normalized SHA-256, not only a version label. Check every claimed deletion/revision against the current text and match approved speech to beat narration before production. Quote the longest stalled span and explain what changes; remove adjacent paraphrases and repeated conclusions. A promised journey or named place alone is not evidence of fulfilled channel grammar.

Mark where the first useful answer and central payoff occur. Review whether each later branch serves that promise or its supported consequence. Classify transitions as causal consequence, prerequisite or next task; require evidence before claiming one solution caused a new problem. Keep material uncertainty in speech, source-access administration in notes, and close once the answer is paid. These are editorial judgments, not automated or audience measurements.
