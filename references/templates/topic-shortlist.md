## Two treatments for the leading candidate

For each A/B treatment: same bounded subject/evidence / distinct viewer question or route / provisional title / thumbnail focal object-action-relationship and optional exact text / native-market search-intent observations and limits / short target-language opening / first useful answer / linked passage contributions / strongest reveal / earned ending / uncertainty and risk. Compare these package concepts before long narration under script-packaging-coordination.md.

Compare accessibility, curiosity, support for the promise, progress and channel voice. Choose with reasons or reframe both. These are editorial alternatives, not a measured A/B test, completed packaging or approved narration. Do not produce full scripts, audio or images just to compare them.
