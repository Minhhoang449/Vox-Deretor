# Documentary research dossier
Record selected channel/topic/slug, live DNA revision and date.
For every essential claim record source URL/title/author/date, exact locator, what was read, the supported statement, scope/definitions, confidence and remaining dispute.
Distinguish primary records, authoritative synthesis, interpretation, source-based reconstruction, illustrative example and optional scenario.
Record event chronology or system flow, competing explanations, human impacts and known aftermath. For institutional claims record jurisdiction, effective date and comparison conditions.
YouTube discovery is a separate dated appendix for audience questions/packaging, never proof or retention analytics.
Reference the candidate's separate native-language/region query ledger and shortlist decision. Classify direct/adjacent long-form, Shorts, wrong-language, unrelated, own-channel and reupload results; record actual inspected passages and original/translated/metadata-only access. Keep audience curiosity, supported-answer feasibility and market observation separate. If routines are connected, record each one's contribution and compatible place/period/social scope.
Optional counterfactual appendix: real baseline, precise divergence, assumptions, resources and conditional inference limits.
