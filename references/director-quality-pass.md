# Directing the approved documentary

Adopted 21/10/2026 after reviewing Lemo-Opuscar. This is a selective adaptation, not an imported renderer or style. Live channel guidance, documentary evidence, narration approval, audio review and original Chibi 2D stills retain authority. Apply to new work or requested revisions; do not reopen approved episodes automatically.

## Intent before pictures

After narration approval, record the actual speech version and passages being directed. Describe what the viewer must understand, the clearest supported visible relationship and how the sequence reaches its payoff. Use the existing two-treatment topic comparison; do not add a compulsory third treatment or rewrite approved speech to fit a demo.

For useful references, state what to learn about composition, legibility or explanatory grammar and what not to copy. Do not inherit an external framework's runtime, voices, style invariants or publication defaults.

## Direct a sequence

Use [visual sequence notes](templates/visual-sequence.md). For important passages record framing, visible action/state, why this viewpoint explains it, its previous/next relationship and needed hold. Preserve place, time, object state and diagram scale. Inspect the actual phone crop rather than enforce a universal subject-size quota.

Inspect a small representative set of actual frames before the rest, such as an opening action, mechanism and ending state when present. Check historical particulars, meaningful human action, identity consistency, legibility and whether the image explains without production notes. Prompts are not verified frames. Keep inspected assets in production when their planned state remains unchanged.

Camera movement cannot animate a hand, tool, cloth or water. Freeze necessary actions in keyframes; changed states need distinct supported images. Do not import moving rigs, a signature-shot requirement, four-move minimum, three-second hook, music-first editing or silence/J-cut quotas. Narration meaning and comprehension set the pace.

## Sound and final review

Use approved voices and BGM from D:/Music. Where useful, record speech priority, ambience/illustrative sound, music state and the reason for a change. Optional foley cannot imply an event or eyewitness recording absent from the evidence. No compulsory sound on every action or cut.

Inspect a contact sheet across the sequence for repeated staging, continuity, missing information and captions over key objects. Inspect meaningful transitions/crops, long diagrams and returning images, then watch/listen at normal speed with sound. Check speech intelligibility, captions and phone readability against the final file. Existing caption/render/reuse validators still apply; passing them does not prove engagement.

Record the file/version, actual spans viewed/listened, defects, corrections and limits. Claim full review only when performed. Repair missing action in the state plan rather than expecting pan/zoom to supply it. Keep editorial readiness, technical validation and audience performance separate.
