# Channel documentary writing

Before delivering draft speech, run `python scripts/draft_review_checks.py <narration.md>` and the six manual questions in ../../references/script-packaging-coordination.md. Include selected title, one-line thumbnail concept, keyword/intent evidence and passage-level visual/source feasibility notes. Quote actual blockers and repairs; inspect source strength and changed scene details manually. The command does not verify facts or approve narration.

Apply [script and packaging coordination](../../references/script-packaging-coordination.md): compare provisional title/thumbnail/opening combinations with the two treatments before long narration, then review the selected package against actual speech before narration approval. Final artwork follows existing gates.

For new scripts and requested revisions, apply [the documentary writing framework](../../references/documentary-writing-framework.md), exposed as live `narration_dna.writing_framework`. It consolidates the existing topic-selection and editorial passes: worthwhile question, supported answer, two treatments, concrete opening, distinct progression and earned ending with native read-aloud review. External storytelling recipes are adapted there; do not load them as replacement authoring authority.

For new work apply ../../references/everyday-ancient-curiosity.md and live narration_dna.topic_scope/question_design. A small everyday question remains the default; a connected set of routines may answer one bounded lived-experience question. Open with an action, object, habit, pleasure, need or contrast; a difficulty is optional. Keep this channel's lens and warm, lightly witty local-language delivery. Exclude the three borrowed example subjects and their paraphrases. Research premises rather than assuming a whole ancient society lacked a modern function.

Read ../../references/scripting-dna.md, ../../references/viewer-guidance.md and ../../references/editorial-review-loop.md, then resolve the channel/topic with channels.py show/context. For any draft, revision or retention review, also read ../vox-viewer-psychology/SKILL.md. Use the live profile's current lanes, story_format, narration_direction, visual grammar and avoid list. This is the active default; old mission and traveler skills are optional historical references, never a requirement.

1. Before proposing/selecting topics, read [topic-selection.md](../../references/topic-selection.md) and run `python scripts/channels.py scout <channel-id>`. Consult [decision memory](../../references/topic-memory.md), then state each lead's plain audience-language question and why its answer is worth hearing before deep research. Research finalists separately in their native markets; keep curiosity, factual feasibility and market evidence distinct. For the leading viable candidate compare two genuinely different treatments with short native-language openings and linked passage contributions, then challenge the choice against premise, evidence, market, runtime and the runner-up. Label a same-author second-pass honestly; do not claim independent review. Use [topic-shortlist.md](../../references/templates/topic-shortlist.md) for recommendation, alternatives and objections/actions. Append serious finalists' decisions with sources; append real publication outcomes separately when available. Do not bind merely to scout or treat memory as approval. After selection, save brief/dossier/claim ledger/story card and keep identity stable.
2. Research public YouTube questions and packaging as discovery; verify important facts and mechanisms using primary/authoritative sources. For institutions or economics record jurisdiction/date/definitions; for disaster and war reconstruct only supported timelines, actions and available information.
3. Plan runtime and a rough speech-unit budget from matching live voice calibration before continuous narration; confirm native-speed TTS later. Use the channel's grammar: food/process history, invention, past daily life, social-rule origins, Japanese human history or human geography. Use the brief's real question; do not impose a hypothetical intervention, success test, traveler or future scenario. Narrative paths guide diagnosis, not compulsory chapters. Keep passage maps and review terminology in notes; write connected spoken explanation with concrete supported detail and natural transitions.
4. Apply Story execution in scripting-dna.md and viewer-guidance.md. Align the title/thumbnail promise with the first image and speech; deliver useful progress by roughly 0:30. Follow what people do and what the viewer newly understands; use the question/evidence map in notes. A connected routine is not automatically a lecture, and a single process is not automatically progressive. Merge repeated qualifications with the same purpose while preserving uncertainty that changes the answer. Distinguish causal consequence, prerequisite and next task. No invented emotion, mandatory difficulty, fixed cliffhanger or interruption quota. Second person can invite an explicitly illustrative viewpoint; it cannot establish a real person's experience or a universal routine.
5. Close once the central promise and relevant consequence or limit are clear. A callback, maintenance section or discussion question is optional when it adds meaning; remove stacked conclusions. A documentary may conclude with an unresolved factual question; do not invent a confident verdict.
6. Use ../../references/channel-benchmarking.md to compare actual passages with the relevant observed samples, identifying original versus translated evidence and local-market gaps. Apply the red-pen, edit fact-check, channel-voice/naturalness and contextual pattern checks in ../../references/editorial-review-loop.md; do not use universal banned-word lists. Run separate story-editing.md and retention-audit.md passes on the full actual version, recording its speech source and normalized SHA-256; verify claimed cuts against the text and question path. Revise major issues, report /16 and /10, quote channel-fit evidence and state text-only limits. After publication, use video-level YouTube retention and compare each signal with the exact passage/image; text scores are not analytics. User approves narration before beats and production. Follow ../../references/narration-review.md to record that actual approval and listen to native TTS before producing all images; never auto-approve a new candidate from a workflow-change request.

A specifically selected counterfactual must identify the real baseline, divergence, assumptions and inference limits under ../../references/historical-fiction.md. The active workflow contains no mission template. A selected war topic adds ../vox-war-scriptwriting/SKILL.md.
