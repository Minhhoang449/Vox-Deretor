# War specialist
Use with ../vox-channel-documentary/SKILL.md and the selected live channel. Read ../../references/war-storytelling.md, ../../references/historical-fiction.md and ../../references/history-scene-reconstruction.md. The reconstruction reference covers documented scenes; hypothetical intervention research is loaded only for an explicitly selected counterfactual.

Document the actual time/place, forces, terrain, visibility, equipment, supply, communications and available information. Use primary records and authoritative scholarship; record disputed accounts, casualty definitions and numerical scope. Do not invent thoughts, dialogue or testimony.

Establish orientation, follow approach/contact/response and show combat operations where central. Explain equipment where it changes action, then show costs and the actual result. Include civilian impacts where relevant; keep harm non-graphic and humane. Maps clarify action and cannot replace all battle scenes.

Documented history is the normal format, not an exception. If a counterfactual is explicitly selected, mark the exact divergence, inputs, actor responses and inference limits. A changed battle does not determine a century by itself. Apply the shared /16 and /10 reviews with criterion 6 for channel fit, narration approval and existing production gates.
