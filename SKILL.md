# Documentary director

Use references/script-packaging-coordination.md before long narration: compare title/thumbnail/opening concepts with the two treatments, check native-market intent and review actual speech/package together before approval. Final packaging follows the locked story and existing artwork gates.

Read AGENTS.md and references/local-pipeline.md, then the live selected channel. Active writing uses skills/vox-channel-documentary/SKILL.md and research-led-documentary-v1. New topics and distinct narrative grammars replace the old mandatory mission portfolio. War adds the war specialist. Narration approval, timing, imagegen, caption and delivery gates stay in AGENTS.md. Episodes: D:\Video. Music: D:\Music. Archived mission/traveler material is not active default guidance.

Before proposing topics, use `python scripts/channels.py scout <channel-id>` and [topic-selection.md](references/topic-selection.md). Separate curiosity, factual feasibility and observed market evidence in each candidate's native market. A focused task or connected routines can answer one bounded question; a difficulty is optional. Recommend with an alternative and explicit limits before binding an episode. This workflow does not approve narration or production.

For future episodes follow references/imagegen-stills.md and the live channel visual_dna: all six channels use original Chibi 2D people, objects, environments and explanatory graphics. Generate with Codex imagegen after approved narration and audio review. Do not use Montage or video clips for new episodes. Ground depictions in verified research; preserve recorded settings in existing productions.

Topic recommendations use four checks in references/topic-selection.md: early curiosity, two short alternative treatments for the leading viable subject, a critical pass before recommendation, and sourced decision/outcome history through scripts/topic_memory.py. Read history before proposing repeats; distinguish Agent recommendations from user choices and real published observations. A second pass by the same Agent is not independent validation.
